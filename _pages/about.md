<div class="about-intro">
  I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics at the <strong>University of Aveiro</strong>, where my research revolves around paraconsistent logic, automata theory, lattice theory, and formal specification. My work primarily focuses on designing formal frameworks capable of analyzing and reasoning about inconsistent or conflicting information in a controlled, sound manner.
  <br><br>
  I completed my MSc in Mathematics and Applications at the University of Aveiro with a dissertation titled <a href="https://ria.ua.pt/bitstream/10773/40670/1/Documento_Juliana_Cunha.pdf" target="_blank"><i>"Towards a Specification Theory for Paraconsistent Transition Systems"</i></a>, following a BSc in Mathematics from the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Porto (FCUP).
